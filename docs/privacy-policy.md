# Privacy Policy — Snowfield (6_IL)

**Last updated: 2026-08-30**

## Overview

Snowfield is a browser and desktop game. This policy describes what data is (and is not) collected.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

Snowfield does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. Specifically:

- No account registration or login required
- No names, email addresses, or contact information collected
- No gameplay telemetry or analytics sent to any server
- No crash reports uploaded automatically
- No advertising identifiers used

## Local Save Data

Village save data is stored **locally on your device only** — in localStorage (web) or local files (desktop). This data never leaves your computer and is not accessible to us.

## Third-Party Services

Snowfield does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or social platforms.

**Steam** (when distributed via Steam): Valve Corporation's [Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/) applies to your Steam account data. We receive no personal data from Steam.

**itch.io** (when distributed via itch.io): itch.io's [Privacy Policy](https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy) applies. We receive no personal data from itch.io.

## Children

Snowfield does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
