## Full Description

<h2>Survive the Snowfield. Grow the Village. Never Forget the Dead.</h2>

Snowfield is a fantasy medieval colony survival game that blends the auto-battle spectacle of Vampire Survivors with the weight of permadeath and village-building. Every dawn you venture into the frozen wilderness. Every dusk you defend what you've built.

<h3>Day Cycle — The Hunter</h3>
When morning breaks, lead your party into the snowfield. Recruit wanderers, hunt massive beasts, and scavenge resources before nightfall. Fog shrouds the unknown. Push too deep and you won't make it back.

<h3>Night Cycle — The Defender</h3>
At sunset, the undead come. Place barricades on a grid, position your companions, and watch the auto-battle unfold. Your allies fight automatically — your job is placement, equipment, and knowing when to hold the line.

<h3>The Village Persists. Your Companions Don't.</h3>
Buildings you construct survive indefinitely — a growing testament to every run. But companions who fall are gone permanently. Who you bring beyond the walls becomes a real decision with real consequences.

<h3>The Great Blizzard</h3>
Rare, catastrophic, and unmistakable. When the mega-blizzard hits, the undead surge alongside killing cold. Every shelter, every fire, every soul you've gathered gets tested at once.

<h3>Features</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Day/night cycle with distinct hunter and defender phases</li>
  <li>Permanent village growth across sessions</li>
  <li>Permadeath companions — loss has real meaning</li>
  <li>Auto-battle defense with strategic unit and barricade placement</li>
  <li>Rare mega-blizzard events that combine every threat simultaneously</li>
  <li>ECS-driven architecture supporting thousands of entities at once</li>
  <li>Fantasy medieval aesthetic with open-license pixel art</li>
</ul>
